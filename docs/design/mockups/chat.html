Asymmetric numbers usually mean the two Thunderbolt ports on the mesh aren't symmetric peers: one direction is riding the port that also carries the Thunderbolt Bridge interface. Re-run the config with auto-setup so the bridge is disabled on the RDMA ports, then probe again.

# regenerate hostfile, disable TB bridge on RDMA ports
mlx.distributed_config --hosts studio-a,studio-b \
  --backend jaccl --auto-setup --output deploy/cluster/jaccl.json
